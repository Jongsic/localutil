Letters outside Latin, Hangul, Han and kana
Normal in a text written in those languages. Anywhere else, a Cyrillic, Greek or Armenian letter that looks Latin is a way to dodge filters and fool readers. Clean turns a letter with a Latin twin into that twin and removes the rest.
